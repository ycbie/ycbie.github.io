About

I am an undergraduate student in economics at Shandong University, where I am enrolled in the Xue Muqiao Honors Program under the National Training Plan for Top Students in Basic Disciplines 2.0.

My research interests are broadly in applied microeconomics, organizations, and the Chinese economy. I am particularly interested in how institutions, technology, and spatial frictions shape individual, household, and firm behavior. I also enjoy using computational and machine-learning methods in economic research.

Research

My current working paper, “Rural School Consolidation and Parental Migration in China,” studies how changes in access to rural public education affect household migration decisions. The project has been presented at undergraduate research forums at Peking University and Renmin University of China.

I have also worked as a research assistant on projects in economic geography and international trade, organizational economics, AI and finance, and text-as-data. See the Research page for details.
