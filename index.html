About Me

Research-oriented economics student

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Economics at the School of Economics, Shandong University.

My research combines reduced-form empirical methods with structural approaches, using micro-level data and quantitative models to study questions related to migration, public services, urbanization, and regional development.

I am currently working on research projects related to rural public education, population mobility, and the spatial allocation of public resources.

Research

Selected projects and research assistance

Rural School Consolidation and Parental Migration in China

Working Paper

This paper studies how changes in rural public education accessibility affect household migration decisions in China, focusing on parental mobility and intra-household spatial arrangements during rural school consolidation.

Migration Education Rural Development Public Goods China

Digital Governance and Fiscal Policy Similarity in China

Research Project

This project constructs text-based measures of policy similarity between central and local governments using government budget documents and natural language processing methods.

Digital Governance Fiscal Policy Text as Data

Platform Markets, Algorithms, and Labor Supply

Research Project

This project examines how platform design choices, including pricing rules, matching algorithms, and incentive mechanisms, affect labor supply and market outcomes in digital markets.

Platform Markets Algorithms Labor Supply
