About

I am an undergraduate student in economics at Shandong University, where I am enrolled in the Xue Muqiao Honors Program under the National Training Plan for Top Talents in Basic Disciplines 2.0.

My research interests are broadly in applied microeconomics, organizations, and the Chinese economy. I also enjoy using computational and machine-learning methods in economic research.
