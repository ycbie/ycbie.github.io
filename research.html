Research

Working Paper

Rural School Consolidation and Parental Migration in China

Working paper

Studies how changes in the accessibility of rural public education affect household migration decisions in China, with a focus on parental mobility and household spatial arrangements during rural school consolidation.

Presented at the Second Undergraduate Forum on New Structural Economics, Peking University (April 2026), and the Second National Undergraduate Forum on Labor Economics and Management, Renmin University of China (May 2026).

Independent / Funded Project

Efficiency of Local Government Debt-Funded Public Services

Shandong University Youth Student Basic Research Program · Jul 2025–present · RMB 40,000

Examines local-government debt allocation and the provision of basic public services under fiscal decentralization, with attention to equal access to services for residents and spatial heterogeneity in policy effects.
